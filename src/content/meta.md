What you stumbled upon is **a new lens** to look at how people move in the experiences you craft for them. By the time you gobble up all of the information here, you will be able to make sense of previously enigmatic motions of the masses by modeling the player journey. The premise: instead of *scooping water from where it pools to where we want it*, we will obtain the tools needed to **move the riverbed**.
