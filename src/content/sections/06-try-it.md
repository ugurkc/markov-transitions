Prefer to just play? [The playground](play/) is this same tool with the writing out of the way and the graph given room to breathe: open it in a new tab and come back whenever.

Otherwise, [everything on the right](#tool:canvas) is already live. Drag states around, rewire transitions, drop players into the simulator, and watch the graphs update in real time. The funnel-with-churn example is loaded by default, but it’s one of four: switch to the [win-back loop](#tool:canvas?preset=preset-winback), the [ranked ladder](#tool:canvas?preset=preset-ranked), or the [free-to-paid funnel](#tool:canvas?preset=preset-monetization): same math, different stakes. Or start from a blank canvas with “Build your own” and wire up a lifecycle of your own.
