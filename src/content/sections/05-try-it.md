[Everything on the right](#tool:canvas) is live for the rest of this page. Drag states around, rewire transitions, drop players into the simulator, and watch the graphs update in real time.

The funnel-with-churn example is loaded by default, but churn is only one question this shape can answer. Switch to the [win-back loop](#tool:canvas?preset=preset-winback) to see what happens when leaving isn’t permanent; to the [ranked ladder](#tool:canvas?preset=preset-ranked), where the states are skill tiers and players climb and slide between them; or to the [free-to-paid funnel](#tool:canvas?preset=preset-monetization), which is the same funnel pointed at revenue instead of retention. Same math each time — only the labels and the stakes change. Or start from a blank canvas with “Build your own” and wire up a lifecycle of your own.

The ranked ladder is worth a second look on its own: every rung is stickier than the one below it, but not by an even amount. A Bronze player who reaches Silver buys themselves about **1.7 extra weeks** of expected time before going inactive. Silver to Gold buys **0.6 weeks**. Gold to Platinum buys barely **0.2**. The climb keeps paying off — it just pays off less and less the higher someone already is, which is exactly the kind of thing a leaderboard can’t show you and a matrix can.
